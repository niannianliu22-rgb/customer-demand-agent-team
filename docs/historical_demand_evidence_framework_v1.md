# Historical Demand Evidence & Analysis Depth Framework V1

This framework separates three questions that must not be conflated:

1. **Demand Existence** — every valid standardized Task Type that occurred historically remains `HISTORICAL_DEMAND`.
2. **Demand Priority** — frozen P1–P4 labels express business and analysis priority only; P4 is not “non-demand”.
3. **Analysis Evidence / Depth** — Evidence Levels decide the strength of a market conclusion and whether a later, selective drill-down may be considered.

Evidence is rule-based, combining persistence, annual and three-year counts, both distribution shares, direction, stability, denominator size, and—only where relevant—Revenue Value. It is not a one-threshold `LOW_SAMPLE` switch. Country and degree use stricter cross-year evidence because they have few values. School is high-cardinality: three-year presence, cumulative scale, shares and direction are assessed together so a genuinely emerging school is not automatically erased.

`Historical Demand Engine` and the future `Academic Calendar Demand Engine` are independent discovery paths. Their future operational scope is their union. Calendar analysis is not performed in V1 and cannot delete a historical demand.

An Evidence Gate permits only human-approved selective next steps. It never triggers an automatic multi-dimensional cross analysis.
